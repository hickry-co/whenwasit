# WhenWasIt: Food Expiry Tracker

**Privacy Policy & Support** · Last updated: October 30, 2026

WhenWasIt is an iOS app by Hickry that helps you track expiry dates of food, hygiene and household products.

## Privacy Policy

**We do not collect your data.** Hickry has no servers for this app, and we do not receive, store, sell or share any personal information.

- **Your products** (names, dates, quantities, notes, photos) are stored on your device. If iCloud is enabled, they are synced through your own private iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit. We cannot access this data.
- **Home sharing.** If you invite people to a home, the shared data is exchanged between you and the people you invite through Apple's iCloud sharing. Access is managed by you and can be removed at any time.
- **Notifications** are scheduled locally on your device. No notification data is sent to us.
- **Face ID / passcode** is used only to protect the Recently Deleted area. Biometric data never leaves your device and is not accessible to the app.
- **Photos and camera** are used only when you choose to add a photo to a product, and stay on your device (and in your iCloud if sync is on).
- **No analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no third-party SDKs.**

### Deleting your data
Delete products inside the app, or delete the app to remove local data. Data synced to iCloud can be removed from iOS Settings > [your name] > iCloud > Manage Storage.

### Children
The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

### Changes
If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.

# Gizlilik Politikası (Türkçe)

**Verilerinizi toplamıyoruz.** Bu uygulama için Hickry'nin sunucusu yoktur; kişisel bilgilerinizi almaz, saklamaz, satmaz ve paylaşmayız.

- **Ürünleriniz** (isim, tarih, miktar, not, fotoğraf) cihazınızda saklanır. iCloud açıksa kendi özel iCloud hesabınız üzerinden Apple CloudKit ile senkronize edilir. Bu verilere erişimimiz yoktur.

- **Face ID / parola** yalnızca Son Silinenler bölümünü korumak için kullanılır. Biyometrik veriler cihazınızdan çıkmaz.

**Verilerin silinmesi:** Ürünleri uygulama içinden silebilir veya uygulamayı kaldırabilirsiniz. iCloud'daki veriler Ayarlar > [adınız] > iCloud > Depolamayı Yönet bölümünden silinebilir.
